# Boxer AI store listing draft

Publisher must approve claims and complete the account-specific Play Console forms before submission. This draft does not establish a published listing or accepted declaration.

**App name:** Boxer AI

**Short description:** Boxing session feedback, training drills and a round timer.

**Full description:**

Build your boxing practice with a round timer, a local boxer profile and a private session history. Set rounds, work periods and rest periods, then hear a chime as your workout progresses.

Choose photos or write session notes to request optional AI coaching. Review estimated feedback on stance, offense, defense, footwork and combinations, then practice recommended drills. Photo feedback is based on still images; it cannot measure a whole fight or count actual punches. Video analysis is not available in this release.

Browse publisher-maintained gym, fighter and tournament directories. Community records are separate from your private local profile.

Before sending an AI request, review and confirm the data disclosure. You can use the profile and timer without submitting coaching data. Delete the app's saved profile, injury notes, coaching history and timer sessions from Profile. No Boxer account is required; optional paid-service access is verified through your Google Play installation.

Boxer AI provides general fitness information. AI feedback can be inaccurate and is not medical care, diagnosis or treatment. Work with a qualified coach and consult a health professional about injuries or exercise safety.
